ME3592 — Unit III Solved Problems

R-2021 • Tolerance Analysis • Full-working practice

Scope: tolerancing, interchangeability, selective assembly, limits and fits, IS 919 table problems, limit gauges, tolerance analysis, process capability, tolerance stack-up and tolerance charting.
Important: These are instructional practice problems unless explicitly identified as a verified university question. IS 919 values, gauge tolerances and standard dimensions must come from the applicable authorized table/data supplied with the problem.

Problem 1 — Classify a Hole-Shaft Fit

Given:
Hole limits = 40.000 to 40.025 mm
Shaft limits = 39.980 to 39.995 mm.
Step 1 — Maximum clearance
Cmax = Hmax − Smin = 40.025 − 39.980 = 0.045 mm
Step 2 — Minimum clearance
Cmin = Hmin − Smax = 40.000 − 39.995 = 0.005 mm
Step 3 — Classification
Since the minimum clearance is positive, clearance exists for every permissible combination.
Final answer: Cmax = 0.045 mm; Cmin = 0.005 mm; therefore the fit is a clearance fit.

Problem 2 — Tolerance and Deviation from Limits

Given: Basic size = 50.000 mm; upper limit = 50.030 mm; lower limit = 50.010 mm.
Step 1 — Total tolerance
Tolerance = Upper limit − Lower limit = 50.030 − 50.010 = 0.020 mm
Step 2 — Upper deviation
Upper deviation = 50.030 − 50.000 = +0.030 mm
Step 3 — Lower deviation
Lower deviation = 50.010 − 50.000 = +0.010 mm
Final answer: Tolerance = 0.020 mm; upper deviation = +0.030 mm; lower deviation = +0.010 mm.

Problem 3 — Tolerance Stack-Up: Worst Case

Given: Three dimensions in a linear chain are A = 20.00 ±0.02 mm, B = 35.00 ±0.03 mm and C = 15.00 ±0.01 mm. The total dimension is A + B + C.
Step 1 — Nominal total
Tnom = 20.00 + 35.00 + 15.00 = 70.00 mm
Step 2 — Maximum total
Tmax = 20.02 + 35.03 + 15.01 = 70.06 mm
Step 3 — Minimum total
Tmin = 19.98 + 34.97 + 14.99 = 69.94 mm
Step 4 — Total worst-case tolerance
±(0.02 + 0.03 + 0.01) = ±0.06 mm
Final answer: Total = 70.00 ± 0.06 mm; limits = 69.94 to 70.06 mm.

Problem 4 — RSS Stack-Up for Independent Variation

Given: Independent dimensional tolerances are ±0.02 mm, ±0.03 mm and ±0.01 mm.
Formula
TRSS = √(0.02² + 0.03² + 0.01²)
Arithmetic
0.02² = 0.0004
0.03² = 0.0009
0.01² = 0.0001
Sum = 0.0014
√0.0014 = 0.0374 mm.
Final answer: RSS equivalent tolerance ≈ ±0.0374 mm, subject to the independence assumptions of the RSS model.

Problem 5 — Process Capability

Given: Process standard deviation σ = 0.010 mm. Specification limits are USL = 50.030 mm and LSL = 49.970 mm. The process mean is centred at 50.000 mm.
Step 1 — Specification width
USL − LSL = 50.030 − 49.970 = 0.060 mm
Step 2 — Calculate Cp
Cp = USL − LSL6σ = 0.0606 × 0.010 = 1.00
Step 3 — Calculate Cpk
Cpk = min(USL − μ3σ, μ − LSL3σ)

Upper side = 0.030/(3 × 0.010) = 1.00.

Lower side = 0.030/(3 × 0.010) = 1.00.

Therefore Cpk = 1.00.

Final answer: Cp = 1.00 and Cpk = 1.00 for the stated centred process.

Problem 6 — Fit Identification Method Using IS 919

Problem type: A standard fit designation is given, for example a basic size with a specified hole/shaft position and tolerance grade.
  1. Write the basic size.
  2. Identify whether the feature is a hole or shaft.
  3. Read the fundamental deviation from the authorized IS 919 table.
  4. Read the tolerance grade value from the applicable table.
  5. Calculate upper and lower deviations.
  6. Determine maximum/minimum limits.
  7. Calculate clearance or interference limits.
  8. Classify the resulting fit.
Do not guess the table values. The numerical values depend on the specified basic-size range, tolerance grade and position. Use the actual authorized table supplied/available for the examination.

Problem 7 — Limit Gauge Design Method

Given in this problem type: workpiece limits plus gauge tolerance, wear allowance and the applicable gauge-design rule.
  1. Write maximum and minimum workpiece limits.
  2. Identify whether the gauge is for a hole or shaft.
  3. Apply the stated gauge tolerance and wear allowance.
  4. Determine GO gauge limit for the maximum-material condition.
  5. Determine NO-GO gauge limit for the corresponding checking condition.
  6. Round/adopt dimensions only according to the specified gauge-design rule.
  7. Check the resulting gauge limits against the workpiece limits.
Exam discipline: A gauge-design answer is incomplete if it gives a number without showing the workpiece limits, gauge tolerance/wear assumptions and governing rule.

Quick Revision

  • Clearance: hole − shaft is positive.
  • Maximum clearance = maximum hole − minimum shaft.
  • Minimum clearance = minimum hole − maximum shaft.
  • Worst-case stack-up adds absolute tolerances.
  • RSS is appropriate only when its assumptions are satisfied.
  • Cp measures potential capability from specification width.
  • Cpk also accounts for process centring.
  • IS 919 numerical values must be looked up, not invented.
  • GO/NO-GO gauge design must follow the specified gauge-design rules.
