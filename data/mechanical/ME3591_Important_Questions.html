High-Priority Revision by Unit

Unit I: combined loading, failure theories, eccentric loading, stress concentration, fatigue and endurance limit.
Unit II: shaft strength/rigidity, keys, splines, critical speed and couplings.
Unit III: threaded fasteners, eccentric bolted joints, knuckle/cotter joints, welded joints, riveted joints and bonded joints.
Unit IV: helical/concentric/laminated/rubber springs, surge, flywheel energy/stresses, connecting rods and crankshafts.
Unit V: sliding/rolling bearings, hydrodynamic journal bearings, Sommerfeld number, Raimondi–Boyd graphs, rolling-bearing selection, seals and gaskets.
