✍️ How to Write a 13-Mark Answer

① Introduction

Give a precise definition and state the purpose of the instrument, method or concept.

② Principle

State the physical/measurement principle and the governing equation where applicable.

③ Diagram

Draw a neat, labelled diagram. Label the important components and measurement path.

④ Working / Procedure

Write the operation in logical numbered steps rather than a dense paragraph.

⑤ Result & Applications

State interpretation, applications, advantages and limitations, then finish with a short conclusion.

🧮 Numerical

Given → Required → Formula → Substitution → Calculation → Unit → Final answer.
