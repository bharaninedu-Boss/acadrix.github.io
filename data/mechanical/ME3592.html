ME3592 Metrology and Measurements – R2021

Anna University • B.E. Mechanical Engineering • Semester 5

Complete ACADRIX study material covering measurement fundamentals, linear and angular measurement, tolerancing, surface metrology and advanced measurement methods.

🎯 Exam preparation format
All units use 2-mark + 12-mark preparation, with emphasis on full-working 12-mark answers: definition/principle → detailed explanation → labelled diagram → formula/derivation or numerical working → applications → advantages/limitations → conclusion.
The supplied university papers use 2-mark Part A and 13-mark Part B. ACADRIX uses 12M as a study/practice format and does not present it as the official paper mark value.
Previous-year QPs solved: The three supplied ME3592 papers — Nov/Dec 2023, Apr/May 2024 and Nov/Dec 2024 — are now organized question-by-question with year + unit + solved answer in the dedicated QP page.
