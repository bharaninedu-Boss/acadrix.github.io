8. Symbols & Checks

P = load/power depending on context
A = area; M = bending moment; T = torque
d = diameter; D = mean spring-coil diameter or pulley diameter as defined
Z = section modulus; Zp = polar section modulus
σ = normal stress; τ = shear stress
Sut = ultimate tensile strength; Se = corrected endurance limit
W = load/force; N = rotational speed in rpm unless otherwise stated
k = spring stiffness; δ = spring deflection; G = modulus of rigidity
f = coefficient of friction; η = efficiency
