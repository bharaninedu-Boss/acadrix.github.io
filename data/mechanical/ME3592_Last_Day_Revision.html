3. Rapid 2-Mark Revision

  1. Define metrology and measurement.
  2. What is SWIPE?
  3. Differentiate accuracy and precision.
  4. Define systematic and random error.
  5. Define Type A and Type B uncertainty.
  6. What is calibration?
  7. State the principle of air gauging.
  8. Define comparator.
  9. State the principle of a sine bar.
  10. What is an autocollimator?
  11. Define interchangeability and selective assembly.
  12. Differentiate clearance, transition and interference fits.
  13. State Taylor's principle.
  14. What is process capability?
  15. Define datum and GD&T.
  16. Differentiate straightness, flatness and roundness.
  17. Define surface roughness, waviness and lay.
  18. State the principle of a laser interferometer.
  19. What is a CMM?
  20. What is machine vision?

4. 12-Mark Answer Targets

Unit I

Errors and uncertainty with a full worked estimation problem; measurement system analysis and calibration; air gauging.

Unit II

Construction/working of an angular or optical instrument; sine bar/autocollimator; linear instruments; screw-thread or gear measurement.

Unit III

Limits and fits with table-based problem; limit gauges and Taylor principle; tolerance stack-up and process capability.

Unit IV

GD&T and datums; inspection of form deviations; stylus/optical surface measurement; 3D surface metrology.

Unit V

Laser interferometer; CMM construction/types/probes/software/applications; machine vision; CT/white-light scanning.
