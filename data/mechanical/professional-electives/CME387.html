What to learn for long answers

  1. Principle
  2. Neat labelled diagram
  3. Construction / equipment
  4. Working
  5. Important process parameters and their effects
  6. Advantages
  7. Limitations
  8. Applications

Exam habit: For a 12/13-mark process question, do not write only the definition. Build the answer in the above order and draw the standard diagram early.
