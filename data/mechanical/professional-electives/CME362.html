Long-answer structure

  1. Definition / introduction
  2. Neat labelled diagram or flowchart
  3. Principle / methodology
  4. Step-by-step explanation
  5. Important equations or performance indicators
  6. Energy-saving measures
  7. Advantages, limitations and applications

Exam habit: For energy-conservation questions, show the energy flow clearly and separate measurement, analysis and conservation measures.
