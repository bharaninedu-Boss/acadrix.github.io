CME372 — CAD and CAE

R-2021 • B.E. Mechanical Engineering • Professional Elective

Vertical 72-0-2-330 Theory + 30 Lab Periods

Computer-aided design and engineering covering computer graphics, geometric modelling, visual realism, CAD standards, finite element analysis and nonlinear analysis.

Syllabus verified: CME372 is listed under Computational Engineering in the R-2021 Mechanical Engineering curriculum. The course has five theory units plus a CAD & CAE laboratory component.

Unit-wise Notes

Unit I — Fundamentals of Computer Graphics

CAD design process, computer graphics, coordinate systems, 2D/3D transformations, point/line/circle drawing algorithms, clipping, viewing transformation and graphics standards.

CAD & CAE Laboratory

  • Piston-cylinder assembly and motion study.
  • Connecting rod and crankshaft modelling/simulation.
  • Two-cylinder engine assembly.
  • Coupled structural/thermal analysis.
  • Harmonic, transient and spectrum analysis.
  • Buckling analysis.
