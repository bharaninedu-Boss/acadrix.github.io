Long-answer structure

  1. Definition / introduction
  2. Neat labelled diagram
  3. Construction or components
  4. Working / operation
  5. Important features or parameters
  6. Advantages and limitations
  7. Applications / examples

Exam habit: For a 12/13-mark automobile-system question, draw the standard diagram early and explain the working in a clear sequence.
