Primary syllabus authority: Anna University R-2021 B.E. Mechanical Engineering curriculum PDF.

The official curriculum is the authority for examination scope. Third-party syllabus pages and industry websites are used only to cross-check or enrich explanations; they do not replace the university curriculum.

Verified CME344 syllabus scope:
  • Unit I: Introduction to PLM; need and opportunities; EDM, PDM, cPDm, CPC and PLM; PLM/PDM infrastructure, network and communications, data management, heterogeneous data sources and applications.
  • Unit II: Data vault/document management, workflow/process management, product structure management, product classification/programme management, communication/notification, data transport/translation, image services, system administration and application integration.
  • Unit III: Teamcenter, Windchill, ENOVIA, Aras PLM, SAP PLM, Arena, Oracle Agile PLM and Autodesk Vault; PLM architecture and software-selection criteria.
  • Unit IV: PLM selection and implementation case studies, visioning, strategy, feasibility, change management, financial justification, barriers, ten-step approach and benefits.
  • Unit V: PLM customization, EAI/middleware and integration with legacy databases, CAD, SLM and ERP.
