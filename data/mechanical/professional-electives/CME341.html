How to score well

  1. Start every long answer with the design/manufacturing problem.
  2. Draw a neat labelled sketch wherever a feature or redesign is discussed.
  3. Explain the principle in short, logical steps.
  4. Show the design change and state why it improves manufacture/assembly/cost/energy/recyclability.
  5. End with advantages, limitations or applications when relevant.
