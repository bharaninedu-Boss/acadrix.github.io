ME3592 — Important Questions

R-2021 • Metrology and Measurements • Semester V

Exam-focused question bank built from the prescribed Unit I–V scope and the supplied 2023–2024 university papers.

Important distinction: Questions labelled Paper theme from supplied source are based on the supplied university papers; the PDFs are not currently published in the repository. Questions labelled ACADRIX priority are study recommendations derived from syllabus coverage and recurring themes; they are not claimed to be official PYQs.
Preparation format: 2-mark + full-working 12-mark practice. The supplied 2023–2024 papers use 13-mark Part B; ACADRIX's 12M format is only a preparation format.

Unit I — Basics of Metrology

ACADRIX priority — 12M

Explain the measurement process, standards, traceability, accuracy, precision and factors affecting measurement (SWIPE).

Paper theme from supplied source — 12M/Part B

Explain classification/types of measurement methods and measurement errors with remedies/control.

ACADRIX priority — 12M

Explain systematic and random errors, uncertainty estimation, Type A/Type B evaluation and statistical treatment with full working.

Paper theme from supplied source — numerical

Work a measurement-uncertainty problem from repeated readings and a calibration certificate, showing mean, Type A, Type B, combined and expanded uncertainty.

ACADRIX priority — 12M

Explain measurement system analysis, calibration and traceability, followed by the principle and working of air gauging.

  • List SWIPE factors.
  • Define calibration and traceability.
  • Differentiate systematic and random error.
  • Define Type A and Type B uncertainty.
  • Define comparator.
  • State the principle of a sine bar.
  • What is an autocollimator?
  • Define interchangeability and selective assembly.
  • Define tolerance and allowance.
  • Differentiate clearance, transition and interference fit.
  • State Taylor's principle.
  • Define surface roughness, waviness and lay.
  • Define Ra and Sa.
  • What is a datum?
  • What is GD&T?
  • State the principle of a laser interferometer.
  • What is a CMM?
  • What is machine vision?
  • What is in-process measurement?

How to Prioritize

  1. First master the verified recurring paper themes.
  2. Then master the ACADRIX priority 12-mark questions covering every syllabus area.
  3. Practise all numerical categories: uncertainty, sine bar, fits, gauges, tolerance stack-up and process capability.
  4. For every 12-mark answer: introduction → principle → labelled diagram → detailed working → formula/calculation → applications → advantages/limitations → conclusion.
