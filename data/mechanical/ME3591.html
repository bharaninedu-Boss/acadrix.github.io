✍️ How to Write a Design Numerical

① Given

List power, speed, load, dimensions, material properties and service conditions.

② Required

State exactly what must be designed: diameter, thickness, number of bolts, spring dimensions, etc.

③ Criterion

State strength, rigidity, fatigue, wear, life or other design criterion used.

④ Calculation

Show the governing equation, substitutions and intermediate values with units.

⑤ Standardize

Where required, select a practical standard dimension/material and state the choice.

⑥ Check

Verify the final design against the specified condition and present the final answer clearly.

✍️ Design-Numerical Writing Formula

Given → Required → Assumptions → Material/property data → Loading → Governing failure/design criterion → Formula → Substitution → Trial dimension → Standardize → Strength/deflection/life check → Final answer.

For spring problems, explicitly identify mean diameter, wire diameter, spring index, active coils, total coils, free length and solid length. For bearings, keep load, speed, life and catalogue/data-book values consistent.
