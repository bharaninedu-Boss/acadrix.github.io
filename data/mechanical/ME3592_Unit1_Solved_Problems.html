ME3592 — Unit I Solved Problems

Step-by-step practice for Basics of Metrology.

Problem 1 — Mean, deviation and standard deviation

Given: Five repeated readings are 20.02, 20.01, 20.03, 20.02 and 20.02 mm.

Find: Mean and sample standard deviation.

Step 1 — Mean
x̄ = (20.02 + 20.01 + 20.03 + 20.02 + 20.02) / 5
= 100.10 / 5
= 20.02 mm
Step 2 — Deviations from mean

0.00, −0.01, +0.01, 0.00, 0.00 mm.

Step 3 — Squares of deviations

0, 0.0001, 0.0001, 0, 0 mm².

Step 4 — Sample standard deviation
s = √[Σ(xᵢ − x̄)² / (n − 1)]
= √(0.0002 / 4)
= √0.00005
≈ 0.00707 mm
The readings are tightly grouped around 20.02 mm, so the experimental scatter is small.

Final answer: Mean = 20.02 mm; sample standard deviation ≈ 0.0071 mm.

Problem 2 — Standard uncertainty of the mean

Given: The sample standard deviation from repeated observations is 0.00707 mm and n = 5.

Find: Standard uncertainty of the mean.

Step 1 — Use the repeated-observation relation
u(x̄) = s / √n
Step 2 — Substitute
u(x̄) = 0.00707 / √5
≈ 0.00316 mm

Final answer: Standard uncertainty of the mean ≈ 0.0032 mm.

Problem 3 — Combined standard uncertainty

Given: Three independent standard uncertainties are u₁ = 0.02 mm, u₂ = 0.03 mm and u₃ = 0.01 mm.

Find: Combined standard uncertainty.

Step 1 — Use root-sum-square
uc = √(u₁² + u₂² + u₃²)
Step 2 — Substitute
uc = √(0.02² + 0.03² + 0.01²)
= √(0.0004 + 0.0009 + 0.0001)
= √0.0014
≈ 0.0374 mm

Final answer: Combined standard uncertainty ≈ 0.037 mm.

Exam method for uncertainty problems

  1. Write all input quantities and their uncertainties.
  2. Identify whether each component is Type A or Type B.
  3. Convert all quantities to consistent units.
  4. Apply the correct uncertainty propagation relation.
  5. Calculate the combined standard uncertainty.
  6. If requested, apply the stated coverage factor to obtain expanded uncertainty.
  7. Round the final uncertainty sensibly and report the measurement with the same decimal place.

Common mistakes

  • Using n instead of n − 1 for a sample standard deviation.
  • Confusing error with uncertainty.
  • Adding independent uncertainties directly instead of using the specified propagation method.
  • Mixing mm and μm.
  • Reporting more digits than the uncertainty supports.
