🧮 4. Numerical Attack List

  1. Write Given data with units.
  2. Write To Find.
  3. Draw the FBD/sketch if needed.
  4. Convert to consistent units.
  5. Write the governing formula.
  6. Substitute every actual value.
  7. Show arithmetic and intermediate values.
  8. Continue through the next formula instead of jumping to the result.
  9. Check strength/rigidity/safety.
  10. Adopt a practical standard dimension where required and re-check.

ACADRIX numerical standard: Given → Formula → actual substitution → arithmetic → intermediate result → next formula → substitution → final answer.

  • Define endurance limit.

Unit II

  • Why are keys used?
  • What is critical speed?
  • Differentiate rigid and flexible coupling.
  • Why check key crushing?

Unit III

  • Define cotter joint.
  • What is a knuckle joint?
  • Why is fillet-weld throat 0.707s?
  • State one advantage of a power screw.

Unit IV

  • What is spring surge?
  • State the function of a flywheel.
  • What is spring stiffness?
  • Why is a connecting rod designed for fluctuating load?

Unit V

  • Define hydrodynamic lubrication.
  • What is Sommerfeld number?
  • What is a rolling-contact bearing?
  • State the purpose of a seal.
